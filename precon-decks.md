# ManaPool Precon Decks

Every player needs a Field, so only tribes with a Field get a precon (5 decks; Druid Grove was removed on Oct 20, 2026 because Druids have no Field yet). Each precon is exactly 60 cards: the 39 Precon Essentials, every creature of its tribe, then that tribe's support cards (Anthems, Artifacts, Spells, Incantations) to fill the remaining slots.
